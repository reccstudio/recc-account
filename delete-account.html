Delete your Recc account

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your account and artist profile (name, bio, photo),
  • shared mixes you published, and
  • collaboration songs and recordings stored in the cloud.

Songs and projects saved on your phone stay on your phone. Delete them in the app, or uninstall it.

To prove the account is yours, sign in with the same Google account you use in Recc.

Signed in as

You can also delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account. If you can no longer sign in, email reccstudio@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
